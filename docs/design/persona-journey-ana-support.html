Your Porirua Directory · Help-seeker journey

Ana’s path from a hard week to a short list of help

A use-case story for the public directory — how one person finds kai, counselling, and a way back to work in a single visit.

A

Ana · Porirua · whānau of four

Ana has just lost her job after an issue at work. She is the main provider for her whānau of four, and money is already tight. She needs food this week, someone to talk to, and training or support so she can get back into work.

What she needs

Food / kai Counselling Training / work

Private visit Ana knows the list is private. She does not log in, so nothing is stored online or linked to her — and that lets her look for help without feeling watched.

1

Opens the webpage

She opens the directory with no login and no name. Nothing here will be linked to her. That already feels safer.
