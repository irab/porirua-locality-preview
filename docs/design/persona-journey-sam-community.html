Your Porirua Directory · Community journey

Sam’s path from “I want to belong” to local climate groups

A use-case story for Connect with community — how one person finds environment and climate groups in a single visit.

S

Sam · Porirua · new to the city

Sam has recently moved to Porirua and wants to meet people through something that matters. They are looking for local environment groups and climate mahi — planting, harbour care, or a citizens’ assembly — not emergency support.

What they want

Community Environment Climate action
