About Your Porirua Directory

Your Porirua Directory helps people in Porirua find support and connect with local community organisations. Choose Find support to look by topic (for example food, housing, or wellbeing) or search by name. Choose Connect with community to find marae, groups, and other local organisations.

Community listings come from the Porirua Locality Community Connections Map. Other listings come from the NZ Family Services Directory for the Porirua area.
